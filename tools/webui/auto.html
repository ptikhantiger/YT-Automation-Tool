What happens after you click

  1. Your script is saved to GitHub, and GitHub's machines start working. You can close this page and turn off your computer.
  2. They make the voice and captions, pick a matching clip for every sentence, and render the video.
  3. It's uploaded to your channel as private, scheduled for your time.
  4. Then open YouTube Studio: add the thumbnail, check the title, watch it once.
  5. If many clips were weak matches, it's uploaded without the schedule, so it can't go public unchecked. You'll see "Needs your review" below.

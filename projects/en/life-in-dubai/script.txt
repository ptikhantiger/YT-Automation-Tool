THE HIDDEN SIDE OF A CITY BUILT IN THE DESERT

A Dubai most tourists never see.

Beyond the highways.
Behind the resorts.
Under the heat.

THE HEAT
During the hottest months, Dubai changes its behavior.
People plan their day around the temperature.
Outdoor work becomes more difficult.
Air conditioning becomes essential.
Cars become temporary shelters from the heat.
Shopping malls become places to escape the weather.
And even simple activities such as walking can feel completely different.
Dubai's summer climate can reach the mid-40s Celsius.
That is not the kind of heat you simply ignore.
It changes how the city functions.
People move between air-conditioned spaces.
Buildings are designed around cooling.
Cars are cooled before people enter them.
And indoor spaces become extensions of everyday life.
This is the first secret of surviving Dubai.
You do not defeat the heat.
You build your entire routine around it.
